## One-line positioning

BairesDev’s Q3 2025 Dev Barometer shows developers adopting AI faster than most organizations can operationalize it: engineers are saving time, learning quickly, and reporting more fulfilling work, while client companies remain early in AI maturity.

## Suggested report description

In July 2025, BairesDev surveyed 1,129 developers and 50 project managers working across 125 software projects in 63 countries. The Q3 Dev Barometer captures a market in transition: developers are already realizing measurable productivity gains from AI, saving an average of 7.3 coding hours per week, while many organizations still lack mature AI strategies, structured upskilling programs, and the infrastructure needed to scale AI safely. The report also connects AI adoption with remote work, career access, developer learning behavior, and client-side adoption barriers.

## Methodology and respondent profile

- Survey conducted: July 2025
- Developer respondent base: 1,129 developers
- Project manager respondent base: 50 project managers
- Projects represented: 125 software development projects
- Total professionals surveyed: 1,179
- Countries represented: 63
- Developer experience: 56% had 8+ years of experience
- Project types: startups, SMBs, and enterprise projects

### 16. AI maturity at client companies

Question: What is the perceived level of AI maturity at your client’s company?

| Maturity level | Description | Share |
|---|---|---:|
| Level 1 | Exploratory | 36.0% |
| Level 2 | Early Pilots | 28.0% |
| Level 3 | Structured Adoption | 21.6% |
| Level 4 | Scaled Adoption | 11.2% |
| Level 5 | Strategic / Transformational | 3.2% |

Deck callouts:

- Average maturity: 2.2 / 5
- 3% of projects consider AI core to delivery.
- 64% of projects are in Level 1 or Level 2.

Maturity definitions:

1. Exploratory: Organization is starting to explore AI with limited research and small-scale experiments, but no defined use cases.
2. Early Pilots: AI pilots are underway in non-critical areas, with recognized potential but no formal strategy or consistent resourcing.
3. Structured Adoption: Multiple use cases and pilots exist with a roadmap for expansion, though integration remains fragmented.
4. Scaled Adoption: AI is embedded across business functions with governance, impact metrics, and cross-functional teams in place.
5. Strategic / Transformational: AI sits at the core of business strategy, fully integrated across areas and driving innovation through proprietary or specialized models.

Content angle: Developers are moving fast, while organizations remain early in AI maturity.

## Executive-ready synthesis

The Q3 2025 report shows a widening gap between individual developer readiness and organizational AI maturity. Developers are already using AI to save time, learn faster, code faster, and make work more fulfilling. But client companies remain constrained by privacy concerns, legacy systems, unclear policies, weak AI maturity, talent gaps, and limited formal upskilling. For technology leaders, the opportunity is to convert individual experimentation into organizational capability: clear AI strategy, modern data foundations, structured training, and governance that lets teams move faster without increasing delivery risk.
